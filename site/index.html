01 · About

Still writing code.

The short version

A developer who ended up in the boardroom and kept writing code.

I’ve spent my career building software and leading the people who build it. I’ve been at Data8 for 15 years and I’m currently its Chief AI Officer, but I still spend a good part of my week writing code. My work covers data, AI and business applications, and more and more of it now involves AI agents: what they can be trusted to do, which tools and plugins they need, and how they connect to the systems a business already runs on. I’m a six-time Microsoft MVP, currently awarded in both Business Applications and Microsoft 365. I can talk through architecture with a development team in the morning and investment and risk with a board in the afternoon, and I enjoy both.

Outside work I’m a Pokémon fan, and a lot of my spare time goes into running community events.

02 · What I work on

Data, AI and the systems around them.

01 / DATA

Data & integration

Clean, validated, well-connected data. Most AI and automation projects succeed or fail on this long before anyone chooses a model.

02 / AI

AI & agents

I design data-input agents to reduce manual entry, speed up processing and catch errors. Those are goals to test against the real process, not promises made in advance.

03 / EXTENSIBILITY

Plugins & integration

Extending business processes across whatever stack is already in place. That includes over a decade of Power Platform and Dynamics 365, alongside Azure, .NET, Microsoft 365 and third-party APIs.

Standards that travel.

I also build custom developer skills that guide developers and enforce coding standards as they work. They encode practices refined through decades of software work, helping teams keep a consistent style across projects and repositories as tools and frameworks change.
