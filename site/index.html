The short version

Technical enough to build it. Experienced enough to ask whether we should.

I’m Matt, a hands-on developer, technology leader and six-time Microsoft MVP. My career includes 15 years at Data8, where I’m currently Chief AI Officer, but the thread running through it is bigger than any one role: I enjoy solving difficult problems, building useful things and helping people understand what technology can actually do for them. I work across Data, AI and Power Platform, with an understanding of AI agents, plugins and the integrations that connect them to real business systems. I’m just as comfortable discussing architecture and code with developers as I am exploring investment, priorities and risk with a board. I like turning complex ideas into clear conversations, getting stuck into the implementation, and sharing what I learn through the communities and events I help organise.

Away from the job title: a Pokémon fan, a curious builder, and someone who enjoys bringing people together. Serious about the work; happy to have a bit of fun along the way.

02 · The technical playbook

Understanding the technology.
Knowing where it fits.

01 / FOUNDATIONS

Data & integration

Quality, validation and integration. Making data dependable enough for the applications, AI systems and decisions built on top of it.

02 / INTELLIGENCE

AI & agents

Understanding model capabilities and limitations, and how agents use context, tools and workflows to act. Connecting strategy with practical development, evaluation and human oversight.

03 / DELIVERY

Power Platform & plugins

Business applications, automation and custom extensions. Using plugins and integrations to connect capabilities to business processes, with attention to security, reliability and maintainability.

Beyond a good prompt.

I’m interested in how AI becomes part of a working system: which data an agent can access, which tools or plugins it can call, and where permissions, testing and human approval belong. It’s about understanding both the possibilities and the boundaries — then explaining those choices clearly to the people building, approving and using the solution.
